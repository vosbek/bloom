GRANT INTAKE

Record a written reuse permission

Turn the email you have into the repo artifacts: the transcription file, the NOTICE.md entry, and the provenance record. Fill every field from what the message actually says — this tool records a grant; it does not create one. The authoritative artifact remains the original message file you commit alongside the transcription.

How it works: complete the fields below, drag the original email file into the provenance box to compute its SHA-256, then copy the three generated files into grants/ and commit both the outputs and the original. The readiness bar tells you if anything that carries legal weight is missing.

1 · The grantor

Who wrote the permission, and what authority do they hold over Blume Corporation's IP?

2 · What is covered

Tick exactly what the message permits. Anything unticked stays under the existing terms.

3 · Rights granted

Tick only the rights the message explicitly grants. If in doubt, leave it out and rely on the verbatim quote below.

4 · Provenance

The header details that make the record verifiable, plus a hash of the original message.

drop the .eml / .msg / .pdf here, or click to choose

5 · Readiness

Green when the record can carry weight. Amber means ask the sender to restate that part before committing.
